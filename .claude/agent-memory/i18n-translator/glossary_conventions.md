## VEKN vocabulary, prepositions, word order

- **"VEKN ID" order**, file-wide default: fr/pt/it keep "VEKN ID", es reverses to "ID VEKN" — "Pas de VEKN ID" · "Sin ID VEKN" · "Sem VEKN ID" · "Nessun VEKN ID". Local clusters override it, so check siblings:
  - `tournament_vekn_id_required_to_register`/`add_player_no_vekn_id`/`vekn_sponsor_to_register_message`: fr "identifiant VEKN", es "ID de VEKN" (with "de"), it "ID VEKN" (reversed).
  - `profile_claim_vekn_*`/`profile_abandon_vekn_*`: file-wide rule.
  - `csv_import_*` follows `og_reference`'s Archon-import sentence (fr/it "VEKN ID", es/pt "ID VEKN"): "d'abord par VEKN ID, puis par email" · "por ID VEKN primero, luego por correo electrónico" · "por ID VEKN primeiro, depois por e-mail" · "per VEKN ID prima, poi per email".
- **"reported/pushed to VEKN"** (org): "sur VEKN" (or "à VEKN" after "transmis à") · "a VEKN" (no article) · "para a VEKN" · "al VEKN". Verb: transmis · reportado · reportado · riportato.
- **The site `vekn.net`** never takes an article: "transmis à vekn.net" · "reportados a vekn.net" · "reportados para vekn.net" · "riportati a vekn.net".
- **"publish to VEKN"** (calendar listing): "publier auprès de la VEKN" (`og_intro` also attests "publient sur VEKN") · "publicar en la VEKN" · "publicar na VEKN" · "pubblicare sulla VEKN" — all take the article. "Already published on the VEKN calendar": "déjà publié sur le calendrier VEKN" · "ya está publicado en el calendario VEKN" · "já está publicado no calendário VEKN" · "già pubblicato nel calendario VEKN". Heading: "Calendrier VEKN" · "Calendario VEKN" · "Calendário VEKN" · "Calendario VEKN".
- **Three verb families coexist for "results pushed to VEKN"** — the report family above; `og_faq_a_vekn_push`'s publier · enviar · enviar · inviare; the calendar-publish family. Pick by nearest sibling. `twda_status_failed` retry: "publier à nouveau sur VEKN" · "enviar de nuevo a VEKN" · "enviar novamente para a VEKN" · "inviare di nuovo al VEKN".
- **VEKN report "write-once"** (submitted once, never auto-updated; only a VEKN admin can fix): "le rapport n'est envoyé qu'une seule fois" · "el informe se envía una sola vez" · "o relatório é enviado apenas uma vez" · "il rapporto viene inviato una sola volta".
- **"official"**: officiel · oficial · oficial · ufficiale. Sponsorship copy points at the generic official, not "the coordinator of your country" (coordinateur/coordinador/coordenador/coordinatore), unless the string is only about the NC role. Sponsorship is neither NC-exclusive nor country-restricted.
- **"who is a VEKN official"**: "qui est/sont officiel(s) VEKN" · "que es/son oficial(es) de VEKN" · "que é/são oficial(is) da VEKN" · "che è/sono un ufficiale/ufficiali VEKN" (it: no preposition).
- **IC/NC/Prince trio order** follows each locale's own ("IC, NC ou/o Prince"), not en's, when they disagree. "Princes" takes -s in fr/es/pt, invariant "Prince" in it.
- `vekn_sponsor_ineligible`: "Seuls les officiels VEKN (IC, NC ou Prince) peuvent parrainer de nouveaux membres. Demandez à un officiel de les ajouter." · "Solo los oficiales de VEKN (IC, NC o Prince) pueden patrocinar nuevos miembros. Pida a un oficial que los añada." · "Apenas oficiais da VEKN (IC, NC ou Prince) podem patrocinar novos membros. Peça a um oficial para adicioná-los." · "Solo gli ufficiali VEKN (IC, NC o Prince) possono sponsorizzare nuovi membri. Chiedi a un ufficiale di aggiungerli."
- **"National/Continental Championship"** — precedent exists only in `og_intro` prose; **the real `<select>` shows literal English whatever the locale** ([[glossary]] footnote ¶¶; `PromoEditModal.svelte`'s `RANKS` list has the same trap). fr lowercases the adjective and, in generic prose, the noun ("le championnat national ou continental"); es/it capitalize both ("el Campeonato Nacional o Continental" · "il Campionato Nazionale o Continentale"); **pt keeps English**. The short badge `promo_rank_national`/`_continental` is genuinely translatable: "National"/"Continental" · "Nacional"/"Continental" · **pt English "National"/"Continental"** · "Nazionale"/"Continentale".
- **VEKN ranking**: "classement international de la VEKN" · "ranking internacional de la VEKN" · "ranking internacional da VEKN" · "ranking internazionale della VEKN". "Counts toward X": dans · para · para · per. "ranking" is a loanword in es/pt/it, fr **classement** — never the `nav_rankings` page-title word ("Clasificaciones"/"Classificações"/"Classifiche"), which only names that page: "Voir les classements" · "Ver clasificaciones" · "Ver classificações" · "Vedi classifiche".
- **"sanctioned"**: sanctionné · sancionado · sancionado · sanzionato. "Quand un événement devient-il non sanctionné ?" · "¿Cuándo deja de estar sancionado un evento?" (idiomatic, not "se vuelve no sancionado") · "Quando um evento deixa de ser sancionado?" · "Quando un evento diventa non sanzionato?"
- **"rule(s)"** in a bare citation ("(rule 3.1)") is a common noun: règle(s) · regla(s) · regra(s) · regola/regole.

## Register, pronouns, mood

- **Direct-address default** (organizer UI): vous · usted · você · tu. Holds for `rooms_empty_state` (it sibling `rooms_add`="Aggiungi sala" is tu, not Lei "Aggiunga"), `promos_empty_state`, `league_no_tournaments_hint`, cap warnings, `promos_negative_stock`, `promo_movement_*`, `promo_own_stock_title` (votre · su · seu · tuo), `organizers_remove_self_warning`, `admin_export_*`. All routine — **not** the offline/force-takeover block, the only place it uses Lei ([[tone_register]]), e.g. `offline_not_locked_nudge` "usi Vai Offline per…".
- **`admin_*` is tu in it, not Lei**: `admin_op_started_hint`="…**controlla** il pannello…" (Lei: "controlli"), `admin_subtitle`="Esegui manualmente…".
- **`tournament_*` player view**: usted (`pregunte`; `tournament_your_score`="Su puntuación") · vous · você · tu. `player_sitting_out` keeps es/it tú/tu.
- **Button-label mood**: fr/es/pt infinitive, it tu-imperative — including `<a>` links, `title=` and aria-labels styled as actions. `promos_select`: "Sélectionner" · "Seleccionar" · "Selecionar" · "Seleziona".
- **Confirm titles ending in "?"** take infinitive + "?" in all four whatever the file register: "Réinitialiser l'horloge de ronde ?" · "¿Reiniciar el reloj de ronda?" · "Reiniciar o relógio da rodada?" · "Reimpostare l'orologio del round?". Titles **without** "?" use it-imperative (`organizers_remove_title`="Rimuovi organizzatore").
- **Descriptive prose sidesteps register.** `raffle_help` is impersonal 3rd person: "Tire au sort des gagnants au hasard dans un groupe de joueurs." · "Sortea ganadores al azar de un grupo de jugadores." · "Sorteia vencedores aleatoriamente entre um grupo de jogadores." · "Estrae vincitori a caso da un gruppo di giocatori." (it "Estrae", **not** the tu-imperative "Estrai" used elsewhere in that cluster). A short list-item reason can use a non-finite fragment ("à parrainer d'abord").
- Bulk-import modals follow `archon_import_description`: "Inscrivez" · "Inscriba" · "Inscreva" · "Iscrivi". Admin labels/buttons: fr/es infinitive, it tu-imperative, pt você-imperative.

## Markdown, callouts, anchors, punctuation

- **Callouts** — NOTE: NOTE · NOTA · NOTA · NOTA. TIP: CONSEIL · CONSEJO · DICA · CONSIGLIO (`pg_*`) / SUGGERIMENTO (first-install). Warning: AVERTISSEMENT · ADVERTENCIA · — · ATTENZIONE.
- **Anchor slugs — two it regimes coexist, don't unify.** fr/es/pt always keep English slugs. it localized them historically, and untouched old keys still do: `#fare-check-in`, `#come-vengono-calcolati-i-punteggi`, `#gestione-dei-mazzi`, `#gestire-un-evento-online-su-discord`, `#riferimento-regole-di-seduta`, `#check-in`, `#ronde-auto-organizzate`. **Reversed 2026-08-09 for it** by explicit instruction (anchors resolve against English slugs): retranslated keys use bare English slugs while headings stay translated — `#deck-management`, `#checking-in`, `#how-are-scores-computed`, `#running-an-online-event-on-discord`. Don't retrofit old keys or extend the reversal unasked; if unsure, check whether the anchor's *target* heading was retranslated — if not, its localized slug still applies.
- **fr uses a regular ASCII space (U+0020) before `:` and `;`**, not a typographic NBSP (codepoint-verified: "Erreur d'authentification : {error}"). es/pt/it: no space.
- **Quoting a sibling button label: mirror en.** If en quotes it, quote in the locale's own marks (fr « », es «», pt “”, it «»); if not, don't. The "Use [ButtonTerm] for X" shape is always unquoted, the term being the imperative's object: "Utilisez Abandonner pour…" · "Use Retirarse para…" · "Use Desistir para…" · "Usa Abbandona per…"; "Utilisez Modifier pour…" · "Use Anular para…" · "Use Substituir para…" · "Usa Sovrascrivi per…"; "utilisez Passer hors ligne pour…" · "use Modo sin conexión para…" · "use Ficar offline para…" · "usi Vai Offline per…". Distinct from [[tone_register]]'s quoted-label rule, which covers prose *describing* a button.
- **Markup drifts too**: en moving from `"Checked-in"` to `**Checked in**` forces every locale to swap quotes for bold.
- **Bold only what en bolds.** A named tab bolds its key's word ("la liste **Joueurs**" · "la lista **Jugadores**" · Jogadores · Giocatori); the generic phrase stays unbolded ("la liste des joueurs" · "la lista de jugadores" · "a lista de jogadores" · "la lista giocatori").
- Digit runs ("10/9/8…"), U+2026 and em dashes aren't translated content. `user_picker_placeholder` and `promos_select` are the only keys with a real "…" instead of "..." — mirror the en source, don't normalize.
- **Partial source change** → retranslate only the changed clause; diff old-vs-new en first (`difflib.SequenceMatcher`).

## Precedent order when coining

1. **Grep the richest precedent first** — a `pg_*`/`og_*` guide body explaining the same concept outranks the [[glossary]] table. `rankings_cat_*_desc` came from `pg_standings`' rating section ("Classements et ratings" · "Ratings y clasificaciones" · "Classificações e Rankings" · "Rating e Classifiche"); `league_mode_hint_*` from `og_reference`'s Standings Modes ("Modos de Clasificación" · "Modos de Classificação" · "Modalità di Classifica"); `csv_import_*` from `offline_go_online_summary`; `twda_*` from `og_faq_a_vekn_push`.
2. **Check sibling keys in the cluster** before any file-wide default; the local sibling wins.
3. **Same term, same translation** inside a new cluster, even where the wider file is inconsistent.
4. When en switches verb mid-string ("register/sponsor" → bare "add"), mirror the switch instead of normalizing back.
5. Coin only when a full-file grep finds nothing.

## Number, gender, agreement

- **Count markers** (flat JSON, no plural system): `joueur(s)` · `jugador/es` · `jogador/es` · `giocatore/i`. Verbs in dynamic-count sentences default to **plural** unconditionally (es "no tienen") — a pragmatic compromise; flag if stricter handling is wanted.
- fr/es/pt lean to `(s)`, it to the slash (`riga/e`, `carta/e`), Italian never adding -s. Not fully consistent in-file (`mesa/s`).
- Where the it noun is invariable but its adjective isn't, mark only the adjective: `promo registrata/e`, `nascosta/e`, `limitata/e`, `estratta/e`.
- **Real code-branched singular/plural** (`raffle_draw_one`/`_many`) = base verb + singularized established plural, not the `(s)` marker.
- "decklist" is feminine in all four: publique(s) · pública(s) · pública(s) · pubblica/pubbliche.
- **"promo" is feminine in all four** despite the -o (cf. la moto). A draft wrongly wrote es "estos promos", it "questi promo"; correct is "estas promos" · "essas promos" · "queste promo". fr's invariable "ces promos" hides the risk — always check promo demonstratives/articles in es/pt/it.
- it never pluralizes English loanwords: "I tuoi Deck", "Deck dei Giocatori", "le promo", "pt GP", and `pg_standings`' singular gloss "game win, victory point, tournament point" where en is plural.
- Unqualified participles default masculine singular: "Reçu par" · "Recibido por" · "Recebido por" · "Ricevuto da"; "Mouvement enregistré" · "Movimiento registrado" · "Movimento registrato" (pt "Movimentação registrada" is fem only because it names that fem noun).
- A round ordinal **mid-sentence is lowercase**: "avant la ronde 1" · "antes de la ronda 1" · "antes da rodada 1" · "prima del round 1". Capitalization in "Ronde {n} terminée." is sentence position, not a proper noun.

## Tournaments, rounds, seating

- **"desk"**: bureau · mostrador · balcão · banco. Never a table word — table · mesa · mesa · tavolo are the VTES game table. Expand to "bureau des inscriptions" · "mostrador de inscripciones" · "balcão de inscrições" · "banco iscrizioni" (it drops the preposition) whenever it means the registration desk, even where en says just "desk".
- **"pool"** (checked-in-but-unseated players, not the VTES resource) stays a bare loanword: "dans le pool" · "en el pool" · "no pool" · "nel pool". Never "file d'attente"/"lista de espera".
- **"live round"** = `rounds_in_progress`: "en cours" · "en progreso" · "em andamento" · "in corso".
- **Clock/timer**: horloge · reloj · relógio · orologio. "extra" time is a loanword in es/pt/it, fr "supplémentaire": "Donner du temps supplémentaire à cette table" · "Dar tiempo extra a esta mesa" · "Dar tempo extra a esta mesa" · "Dai tempo extra a questo tavolo". `timer_clock_stop` (button) vs `timer_policy_clock_stop` (noun) differ in es/pt/it per [[glossary]]; fr identical.
- **"seating"** as the whole chart: placement · disposición · disposição · disposizione.
- **"move"** as a noun: déplacement · movimiento · movimento · spostamento ("Déplacement de {name}"/"Spostamento di {name}"). Announcements: "Déplacement annulé" · "Movimiento deshecho" · "Movimento desfeito" · "Spostamento annullato". Undo: "Annuler" · "Deshacer" · "Desfazer" · "Annulla".
- **Reorder controls**: "Monter"/"Descendre" · "Subir"/"Bajar" · "Mover para cima"/"Mover para baixo" · "Sposta su"/"Sposta giù".
- **remove-from-list/role vs delete-object** are distinct families ([[glossary]]): retirer · eliminar · remover · rimuovere, vs supprimer · eliminar · excluir · eliminare. **es doesn't split.** Config lists (rooms, organizers, promo rows) take remove; permanent assets take delete. it attests "Aggiungi / Rimuovi Tavolo".
- **"no-show"** has no fixed noun in-file; use a predicate adjective: "marqué·s absent·s" · "marcados como ausentes" · "marcados como ausentes" · "segnati come assenti". "Marked as": marqué comme · marcado como · marcado como · **segnato come** (segnare, not marcare). "Reinstated": réintégré · reincorporarse · reintegrado · reintegrato. The *counted* checked-in status there follows the sibling `action_bar_waiting_initial` (enregistré · registrado · registrados · registrato), not the file-wide "check-in is a loanword in es/pt/it" default.
- **"registration cap"** (soft venue limit) uses a ceiling metaphor so it never collides with the hard round "limit" (limite/límite/limite/limite): **plafond · tope · teto · tetto** (masc). Label "Plafond des inscriptions" · "Tope de inscripciones" · "Teto de inscrições" · "Tetto iscrizioni" (it bare-stacks); prose restores the preposition ("son plafond d'inscriptions", "tetto di iscrizioni"). No cap: "Pas de plafond" · "Sin tope" · "Sem teto" · "Nessun tetto". Naming the concept in prose drops the label's "(optionnel)"/"(opcional)".
- **"seats at the venue"** (generic capacity) must not use the game-table seat words (siège/asiento/assento/posto): use places · plazas · vagas · posti (it's "posti" is unavoidable; context disambiguates).
- **Self-registration is reflexive**: s'inscrire · registrarse · registrar(-se) · registrarsi — distinct from the organizer-registers-someone family inscrire/inscribir/inscrever/iscrivere.
- **"in this tournament"** as a bare locative: "dans ce tournoi" · "en este torneo" · "in questo torneo" · "neste torneio". The registration sense collocates differently (à ce tournoi / en este torneo / a questo torneo / neste torneio).
- **"final"** (the round, in a sentence) is **singular** in all four though the heading pluralizes in es/pt/it (Finales/Finais/Finali) — [[glossary]] footnote †: "pas de finale" · "sin final" · "sem final" · "senza finale"; "aucune finale n'a été jouée" · "no se jugó ninguna final" · "nenhuma final foi jogada" · "non è stata giocata alcuna finale".
- **"ranked"** (rating-eligible tournament) reuses the player-level adjective, not the `tfield_rank` noun: Classé/Non classé · Clasificado/No clasificado · Classificado/Não classificado · Classificato/Non classificato. "Top-ranked player": "le joueur le mieux classé" · "el jugador mejor clasificado" · "o jogador melhor classificado" · "il giocatore meglio classificato".
- **"at least N"**: au moins · al menos · pelo menos · almeno.
- **"casual" (house format)**: **décontracté** (fr translates) · casual · casual · casual. Never informale/informal. "House format": format maison · formato casero · formato caseiro · formato casalingo. "Multi-week": multi-semaines · de varias semanas · de várias semanas · multi-settimana. "Drop-in play": "à participation ponctuelle" · "de participación puntual" · "de participação pontual" · "a partecipazione occasionale".
- **Generic UI "warning"** (banner/badge) is the plain word — avertissement · advertencia · aviso · avvertimento — **not** the `sanction_level_warning`/`_caution` taxonomy.
- **"disqualification"** in prose takes the full term (disqualification · descalificación · desqualificação · squalifica), not the DQ abbreviation; guides gloss it "Disqualification (DQ)"/"Squalifica (DQ)".
- **"Standings Adjustment"** full form (`sanction_level_sa`="SA" badge stays short): "Ajustement de Classement" · "Ajuste de Clasificación" · "Ajuste de Classificação" · "Aggiustamento di Classifica".
- **"There is no undo"**, any destructive confirm body: "Cette action est irréversible." (dominant; "Cette action ne peut pas être annulée." attested once) · "Esta acción no se puede deshacer." · "Esta ação não pode ser desfeita." · "Questa azione non può essere annullata."
- **"no X recorded"**: "Aucune ronde enregistrée." · **es uses a hay-construction** "No hay rondas registradas." → "no hay ganador registrado" · "Nenhuma rodada registrada." · "Nessun round registrato." Match the hay pattern for any future "no X recorded/exists", never a bare "ningún".
- **"No X found" / "Search [plural]…"**: Aucun/Aucune…trouvé(e) · No se encontraron… · Nenhum/Nenhuma…encontrado(a) · Nessun/Nessuna…trovato/a; "Rechercher des…" · "Buscar…" · "Buscar…" · "Cerca…".

## Per-locale traps

- **fr** — translates Constructed/Limited, "ranking"→classement, "casual"→décontracté; keeps "console" bare where es translates it. Regular ASCII space before `:`/`;`. "(optionnel)", never "(facultatif)". Sanction levels counter-intuitive (Caution="Avertissement", Warning="Alerte"). No record-vs-save split. Four check-in word families.
- **es** — reverses to "ID VEKN"; hay-construction for "no X recorded/found"; no remove-vs-delete split (eliminar), no link-vs-attach split (vincular); "venta de entradas", not a loanword; leísmo "añadirle" for unknown gender.
- **pt** — "celular" (pt-BR); "estoque" not stock; "pontos de classificação" not "de rating"; "Hall da Fama"; "movimentação" for a ledger movement; "com falha" for failed; no distinct attach verb; still leaves several bold UI labels in English in guide prose. **pt-BR is the file's convention** — a pt-PT sweep fixed ronda→rodada, cronómetro→cronômetro, descarregar→baixar, ligações partilhadas→links compartilhados, predefinido→padrão; don't reintroduce the European forms.
- **pt `tfield_rank` = "Categoria"**, not "Classificação" — the championship tier (Basic / National / Continental), which must not collide with "Classificação" = standings. Same collision-avoidance as fr "Rang" / es "Rango". Unconfirmed against community usage; flag rather than silently re-coin. `promo_rank_national` is "Nacional" (fr's "National"/"Continental" are real French words and stay).
- **it** — never pluralizes English loanwords; slash-marks adjectives, not nouns; "segnare" not "marcare"; TWDA masculine; Lei only in the offline/force-takeover block (`admin_*` included is tu); omits the possessive on "la fotocamera"; unreconciled idoneo/eleggibile split; localized anchor slugs in older keys only.
