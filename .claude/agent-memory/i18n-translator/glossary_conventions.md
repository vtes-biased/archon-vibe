## Coined phrases & ordering precedents
- Clock stop has two keys: `timer_clock_stop` (imperative/button) vs `timer_policy_clock_stop` (noun/policy) — es/pt/it differ per the [[glossary]] "clock stop" row; fr is identical for both.
- "VEKN ID" word order (checked 2026-07-12 by grep frequency across each file, not a single example): fr/pt/it keep English order "VEKN ID" (dominant, e.g. `vekn_no_id` "Pas de VEKN ID"/"Sem VEKN ID"/"Nessun VEKN ID"); es reverses to "ID VEKN" (`vekn_no_id`="Sin ID VEKN"). Don't swap these per-language orders. **Refinement (2026-07-13)**: this is a *file-wide* default, but some local UI sections have their own established sub-convention that overrides it — check sibling keys in the same section before trusting the global count. Confirmed: the `tournament_vekn_id_required_to_register`/`add_player_no_vekn_id`/`vekn_sponsor_to_register_message` cluster uses fr "identifiant VEKN" (translated, not "VEKN ID") and es "ID de VEKN" (with "de", not the file's usual bare "ID VEKN") consistently across all 3 keys — while the `profile_claim_vekn_*`/`profile_abandon_vekn_*` cluster uses fr/es/pt/it "VEKN ID"/"ID VEKN" per the normal file-wide rule. it's `tournament_vekn_id_required_to_register` cluster likewise prefers "ID VEKN" (reversed) even though it's file-wide default is "VEKN ID".
- "desk" (as in "the organizer at the registration desk") must NOT be translated as table/mesa/tavolo — those words are already heavily overloaded in this codebase for the VTES *game table* (fr `table`, es/pt `mesa`, it `tavolo`, 80–107 occurrences each). Use `bureau`(fr)/`mostrador`(es)/`balcão`(pt)/`banco`(it) instead — see [[glossary]] row "registration/check-in desk". Coined 2026-07-13 for `vekn_guidance_new_member` and the `pg_login_reset` sponsor sentence; none of these words collided with existing strings at the time.
- Sponsorship guidance was reworded 2026-07-13 to stop pointing specifically at "the coordinator of your country" (fr coordinateur/es coordinador/pt coordenador/it coordinatore) and instead point at the generic "official" (VEKN official — NC, Prince, or tournament organizer are all examples) — reflects that sponsorship isn't NC-exclusive. When touching sponsor-adjacent copy (`profile_sponsorship_banner`, `community_sponsor_cta`, `pg_login_reset`, `vekn_guidance_*`), prefer "official"/officiel/oficial/ufficiale over "coordinator", unless the string is specifically and only about the National Coordinator role.
- `vekn_sponsor_ineligible` (2026-07-13) dropped the cross-country sponsoring restriction entirely — any VEKN official can now sponsor any new member regardless of country — same trend as the row above. New canonical text: en "Only VEKN officials (IC, NC, or Prince) can sponsor new members. Ask an official to add them." → fr "Seuls les officiels VEKN (IC, NC ou Prince) peuvent parrainer de nouveaux membres. Demandez à un officiel de les ajouter." / es "Solo los oficiales de VEKN (IC, NC o Prince) pueden patrocinar nuevos miembros. Pida a un oficial que los añada." / it "Solo gli ufficiali VEKN (IC, NC o Prince) possono sponsorizzare nuovi membri. Chiedi a un ufficiale di aggiungerli." / pt "Apenas oficiais da VEKN (IC, NC ou Prince) podem patrocinar novos membros. Peça a um oficial para adicioná-los." Trio order "IC, NC, or Prince" here matches en directly (unlike the `og_offline` case below where en's order had to be overridden) — this key's *pre-existing* text already ordered IC-then-NC/Prince, so no conflict arose. The English source switched from "sponsor" to bare "add" for the second sentence (avoiding repetition) — translated literally as the **"Add Player" button verb** (see [[glossary]] "add" row), NOT the register-family verb, even though this string lives in the add-player flow. Rule of thumb: when en text switches from "register/sponsor" to bare "add" mid-string, mirror that switch to the literal add-verb rather than normalizing back to the register-family verb.
- "reported/pushed to VEKN" (the organization, not the vekn.net site) preposition, per `og_faq_q_vekn_push`: fr "sur VEKN" (or "à VEKN" for a "transmis à" verb), es "a VEKN" (no article), pt "para a VEKN" (WITH article — contraction, unlike es), it "al VEKN" (WITH article, a+il contraction). When the target is the *site* `vekn.net` instead, no article is needed in any of the four (it's a domain name): fr "transmis à vekn.net", es "reportados a vekn.net", pt "reportados para vekn.net", it "riportati a vekn.net". Don't conflate the two — "VEKN" (org) takes an article in pt/it but not es; "vekn.net" (site) never takes one.
- VEKN report "write-once" concept (results submitted once, never auto-updated after edits/reopening — only a VEKN admin can fix manually): no prior precedent existed before `finish_confirm_vekn`/`reopen_confirm_vekn_warn`/`vekn_out_of_sync_hint` (added 2026-07-12) — coined one canonical phrase per locale and reused it verbatim across all three keys for internal consistency: fr "le rapport n'est envoyé qu'une seule fois", es "el informe se envía una sola vez", pt "o relatório é enviado apenas uma vez", it "il rapporto viene inviato una sola volta". Reuse these exact phrases for any future VEKN-write-once string rather than re-coining.
- Dynamic-count sentences with a verb (e.g. "{count} player(s) will be ranked and rated") have no established precedent for verb number agreement (flat JSON, no plural system). Convention adopted 2026-07-12: keep the noun-level ambiguity marker per locale (fr `joueur(s)`, es `jugador/es`, pt `jogador/es`, it `giocatore/i`) but default the verb to **plural** form unconditionally (e.g. es "no tienen", not "no tiene/tienen") — a pragmatic compromise, not a confirmed team decision; flag if a stricter approach is ever requested.
- "Reporting/reported" verb choice differs by sense: "reporting VP scores at your table" (`pg_checkin_details` heading "Reporting Results") uses fr "Saisir"(entering)/es "Registro"/pt "Registrando"/it "Riportare"(same word, different sense) — don't reuse that heading's fr/es/pt verb for "reported to VEKN/vekn.net" (external system submission), which instead uses fr "transmis"/es "reportado"/pt "reportado"/it "riportato" (it happens to reuse the same word for both senses; fr/es/pt do not).
- "Decklist becomes public" (`finish_confirm_decks_*`, added 2026-07-12): coined by analogy with the existing "become visible to all members" pattern (`og_faq_a_decklists_mode`: fr "deviennent visibles"/es "se vuelven visibles"/pt "ficam visíveis"/it "diventano visibili") — no prior precedent for "public" specifically. Used: fr "deviennent publiques", es "se hacen públicas", pt "se tornam públicas", it "diventano pubbliche". "decklist" is feminine in all four (la/la/a/la), so agreement is `publique(s)`/`pública(s)`/`pública(s)`/`pubblica/pubbliche`.
- Keep in English (all langs): VEKN, VP, GW, TP, TWDA, VDB, Archon, Discord, Markdown, IndexedDB, QR, Standard, Grand Prix, Inner Circle (IC), NC, Prince, passkey, multideck.
- **Correction (2026-07-13)**: Constructed/Limited are NOT uniformly kept in English — es/pt/it do, but **fr translates them** ("Construit"/"Limité", confirmed in tab labels `rankings_cat_*` and in `pg_standings` prose). Always check the locale's own tab-label keys before assuming these stay English; see [[glossary]] row "Constructed / Limited" and footnote ‡‡.
- `rankings_cat_{constructed,constructed_online,limited,limited_online}_desc` (added 2026-07-13, rankings-page tab captions): the exact phrasing for "rating points"/"best N tournaments over 18 months" was NOT coined fresh — it was pulled verbatim from the pre-existing `pg_standings` help-guide section ("Rating e Classifiche" it / "Ratings y clasificaciones" es / "Classements et ratings" fr / "Classificações e Rankings" pt) which explains the identical rating formula. Deliberately translated en "results" as "tournois/torneos/torneios/tornei" (tournaments) rather than literally, to match that established phrase rather than coin a synonym — see [[glossary]] "best N tournaments" row. **When a new string describes a concept already explained in a help-guide (`pg_*`/`og_*`) body, grep that body first — it's often the highest-authority precedent, higher than reconstructing from the glossary table.**
- NOTE/TIP callout titles per locale: fr NOTE→NOTE, TIP→CONSEIL; es NOTE→NOTA, TIP→CONSEJO; it NOTE→NOTA, TIP→CONSIGLIO (pg_ context) / SUGGERIMENTO (first-install); pt NOTE→NOTA, TIP→DICA. AVERTISSEMENT(fr)/ADVERTENCIA(es)/ATTENZIONE(it) for warnings.
- Anchor link convention: fr/es/pt keep English slugs unchanged; it localizes slugs to match the translated heading (e.g. `#self-organized-rounds` → `#ronde-auto-organizzate`, `#seating-rules-reference` → `#riferimento-regole-di-seduta`).
- `og_force_takeover` was split in en.json: the tail (Archon Import + Leagues + Seating Rules Reference + FAQ heading) moved to new key `og_reference`. When any locale's `og_force_takeover` still contains that tail, it must be trimmed to only the Force Takeover subsection.
- "who is a VEKN official" restriction (added 2026-07-13 to `og_offline` step 2 and `og_force_takeover`, gating Go-Offline/Force-Takeover to Prince/NC/IC because offline mode can mint new members): fr "qui est/sont officiel(s) VEKN", es "que es/son oficial(es) de VEKN" (with "de"), pt "que é/são oficial(is) da VEKN" (with "da"), it "che è/sono un ufficiale/ufficiali VEKN" (no preposition) — per the [[glossary]] "official" row. Listed the trio as "IC, NC ou/o Prince" (not en's "Prince, NC, or IC" order) to match each file's own pre-existing order convention for this trio (see `tournament_new_no_permission`/`err_tournament_create_forbidden`/`league_new_no_permission`, consistent across all 4 locales) — when en and the locale's established order disagree for this specific trio, follow the locale's established order, not en's.
- pt's `og_offline`/`og_force_takeover`/`og_offline_prepare` markdown bodies contain literal untranslated English button labels inside bold markers (`**Go Offline**`, `**Force Takeover**`, `**Go Back Online**`) even though the actual UI buttons are translated (`offline_go_offline`="Ficar offline", `offline_force_takeover`="Assumir controle") — a pre-existing gap, confirmed still present 2026-07-13. Don't silently fix it as a drive-by edit; it's out of scope unless asked. Worth flagging to the team as a follow-up ticket.
- `tournament_playing_latecomer` (added 2026-07-13, shown to an unregistered logged-in member viewing a mid-round tournament): confirms the `tournament_*` player-view section register per-locale — es uses **usted** here (`pregunte`, matching sibling `tournament_your_score`="Su puntuación"), fr **vous** (`vous ajouter`/`demandez`), pt **você** (`Quer jogar?`, no explicit pronoun needed), it **tu** (`aggiungerti`/`chiedi`, per the player-facing-action-UI half of the it split in [[tone_register]]). Used the "add" verb family (glossary "add" row: ajouter/añadir/adicionar/aggiungere), not the register-family verb, since the sentence is literally about the organizer performing the Add-Player action on a latecomer — same rule of thumb as the sponsor-cta precedent above. es used indirect object "añadirle" (leísmo for a person, gender-neutral) rather than "añadirlo/la" to dodge the object's unknown gender.
- "phone" (the physical device, not "mobile"/"cell"): established word per locale, confirmed 2026-07-13 across `pg_install`/`pg_calendar_feed`/`pg_offline_details`/`og_offline`/`og_force_takeover` and reused for `checkin_qr_scan_instruction` — fr **téléphone** (not portable/mobile), es **teléfono** (not móvil), it **telefono** (not cellulare), pt **celular** (not telefone/telemóvel — pt-BR convention, already noted in the top-level system prompt). "Camera" word choice follows each locale's own sibling key rather than a fixed rule: fr/es/pt use a possessive ("votre téléphone"/"tu teléfono"/"seu celular", matching how `checkin_qr_hint` says "votre caméra"/"tu cámara"/"sua câmera"), it omits the possessive and uses a bare definite article ("la fotocamera del telefono"), matching `checkin_qr_hint`'s "la fotocamera" with no "tua".
- "publish to VEKN" (the *calendar-entry* sense — the tournament has a VEKN calendar listing via the Sync-to-VEKN action — distinct from "reported/pushed to VEKN" = results submission (row above) and "registered with VEKN" = that sync action's own past participle) has its own verb+preposition. Established by `vekn_sync_rounds_required` ("Configure the number of rounds before publishing to VEKN"): fr "publier auprès de la VEKN" (config-gating register; `og_intro` narrative prose instead uses "publient sur VEKN" — both prepositions attested, pick by register), es "publicar en la VEKN", pt "publicar na VEKN", it "pubblicare sulla VEKN" — all four take the definite article with "VEKN" here (unlike the site `vekn.net`, which never does). Reused verbatim 2026-07-13 for `tfield_vekn_locked_hint`/`err_tournament_vekn_frozen_field`, rendering "already published on the VEKN calendar" as fr "déjà publié sur le calendrier VEKN", es "ya está publicado en el calendario VEKN", pt "já está publicado no calendário VEKN", it "già pubblicato nel calendario VEKN" — "VEKN calendar" itself is `og_intro`'s own section heading (fr "Calendrier VEKN", es "Calendario VEKN", pt "Calendário VEKN", it "Calendario VEKN").
- "National/Continental Championship" (tournament rank category): translated per-locale precedent lives only in `og_intro` help prose, not the actual UI — see [[glossary]] row "National/Continental Championship" and its ¶¶ footnote for the trap (the real `<select>` always shows literal English regardless of locale). fr lowercases the adjective and, in generic prose (not naming two distinct list options), the noun too ("le championnat national ou continental"); es/it capitalize both words even in generic prose ("el Campeonato Nacional o Continental" / "il Campionato Nazionale o Continentale"); pt keeps the English phrases untranslated. Used 2026-07-13 for `tfield_ranked_no_proxies_hint`/`err_tournament_rank_forbids_proxies`/`err_tournament_rank_forbids_multideck`.
- `organizers_remove_title`/`_btn`/`_self_warning`/`_add_chip` (added 2026-07-13, `OrganizerManager.svelte` confirm modal + header chip): confirmed the remove-from-role verb family (fr retirer/es eliminar/pt remover/it rimuovere) is distinct from the delete-object verb family (`common_delete` fr supprimer/pt excluir/it eliminare) — see [[glossary]] "remove vs delete" row; es doesn't split, both senses use "eliminar". Title mood follows the no-question-mark → it-imperative rule already in [[glossary]] (`players_remove_title` precedent): "Rimuovi organizzatore", not infinitive. **Register decision for `organizers_remove_self_warning`**: this is a routine self-service action (removing your own organizer role, reversible by another organizer), not the heavy offline/force-takeover admin block covered by [[tone_register]]'s it Lei/tu split — treated it as **tu**, by analogy with `profile_abandon_vekn_description` (another self-service "you're changing your own access" warning, which uses tu: "Manterrai i tuoi metodi..."). If a future string in the `organizers_*` prefix turns out to need Lei (e.g. one organizer's action affecting *another* organizer's access), don't assume this precedent carries over — re-check register per [[tone_register]]'s "check sibling keys" rule. "Co-organizer" and "organizer access" terms pulled from the pre-existing `og_co_organizers` help section rather than coined fresh — see [[glossary]] rows.

- Confirm-modal **title** mood (added to, not new as of 2026-07-13 `timer_reset_confirm_title`): titles that end in "?" use **infinitive + "?"** in fr/es/pt/it regardless of file-wide register — confirmed across `finish_confirm_title`/`reopen_confirm_title`/`decks_delete_confirm_title`/`tournament_banner_remove_confirm_title`/`league_delete_confirm` and now `timer_reset_confirm_title` ("Réinitialiser l'horloge de ronde ?" / "¿Reiniciar el reloj de ronda?" / "Reiniciar o relógio da rodada?" / "Reimpostare l'orologio del round?"). This is distinct from the no-question-mark titles noted below (`players_remove_title`), which use it-imperative instead of infinitive.
- "There is no undo" / "this action cannot be undone" canonical short-form (added 2026-07-13 for `timer_reset_confirm_body`, reusing a pre-existing dominant pattern already seen 4× each in `tournament_delete_msg`/`member_delete_warning`/`offline_force_unlock_msg`/`league_delete_confirm`): es "Esta acción no se puede deshacer.", pt "Esta ação não pode ser desfeita.", it "Questa azione non può essere annullata.", fr has two attested variants — "Cette action ne peut pas être annulée." (`tournament_delete_msg`, 1×) vs "Cette action est irréversible." (`member_delete_warning`/`offline_force_unlock_msg`/`league_delete_confirm`, 3×, dominant) — used the dominant "irréversible" form. Reuse these exact sentences verbatim for any future destructive-action confirm body rather than re-coining.
- Timer/round-clock terms (added 2026-07-13, `timer_reset_confirm_*`/`timer_add_table_time`): "clock" = horloge(fr)/reloj(es)/relógio(pt)/orologio(it), matching the existing `timer_*`/"clock stop" glossary row. "table" (physical game table, not the registration desk) = table(fr)/mesa(es)/mesa(pt)/tavolo(it). "extra" (time) stays the bare loanword "extra" in es/pt/it (per pre-existing `timer_extra_time`) but is translated "supplémentaire" in fr (also pre-existing). Register for `timer_add_table_time` (a judge-facing tooltip on per-table time-extension chips, title attribute on a `Button`, not the chip's own visible label): treated as a routine organizer/judge action (not the heavy offline/admin Lei block) and applied the **button-label mood convention** ([[tone_register]]: fr/es/pt infinitive, it tu-imperative) since it's a short standalone action phrase in a `title=` attribute like `timer_start`/`timer_pause`/`timer_reset` siblings, not descriptive running prose — fr "Donner du temps supplémentaire à cette table", es "Dar tiempo extra a esta mesa", pt "Dar tempo extra a esta mesa", it "Dai tempo extra a questo tavolo" (tu-imperative of dare).
- `rooms_empty_state`/`rooms_validation_hint`/`rooms_move_up`/`rooms_move_down`/`rooms_remove` (added 2026-07-13, `TableRoomsEditor.svelte`, organizer venue-room config for table seating): "seating" here (the overall chart, not individual seat positions — cf. `rounds_print_seating` not `rounds_alter_seating`) uses the "disposición"(es)/"disposição"(pt)/"disposizione"(it)/"placement"(fr) rendering, per [[glossary]]'s seating row. `rooms_remove` follows the remove-from-list verb family (retirer/eliminar/remover/rimuovere), not the delete-object family — a room list is a config list like the "remove organizer" pattern (`organizers_remove_*`), not a permanent asset like a banner; also matches the pre-existing `og_round_start_details` guide text "Aggiungi / Rimuovi Tavolo". `rooms_move_up`/`rooms_move_down` (icon-button aria-labels, no prior reorder-button precedent existed anywhere in the file) coined as fr "Monter"/"Descendre", es "Subir"/"Bajar", pt "Mover para cima"/"Mover para baixo", it "Sposta su"/"Sposta giù" — reuse these for any future list-reorder controls rather than re-coining. `rooms_empty_state` is a direct-address question+imperative; treated as formal vous/usted/você (file-wide organizer-UI default) but **tu** for it, since the immediate sibling `rooms_add`="Aggiungi sala" is already tu-imperative (not Lei "Aggiunga") — this rooms_* section is routine organizer config, not the heavy offline/admin Lei block.
