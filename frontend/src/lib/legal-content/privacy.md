Archon is a free, non-commercial tool for organising and recording Vampire: The Eternal Struggle (VTES) tournaments and leagues, run as part of the Vampire: Elder Kindred Network (VEKN) organised-play programme. The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

> **Black Chantry Productions Limited**
> 13 East Crescent, Beeston, Nottingham, NG9 1PZ, United Kingdom
> orgplay@blackchantry.com

This policy explains what we process, why, and the rights you have under the UK GDPR (and, where it applies, the EU GDPR). For any privacy request, contact **orgplay@blackchantry.com**.

## What we collect

- **Account & sign-in** — depending on how you sign in: your email address, a password or passkey credential, and/or your Discord account ID and the token Discord issues. Some sign-in methods don't require an email at all.
- **VEKN profile** — your VEKN ID, name and profile details, synchronised from vekn.net.
- **Tournament activity** — the events and leagues you join, your results and rankings, decks you submit, and any sanctions recorded against you.
- **Location** — the city and time-zone you set for events (resolved against the GeoNames database).
- **Technical logs** — IP addresses and request metadata kept briefly in server logs for security and debugging.

We do **not** collect special-category data, and we do **not** carry out any automated decision-making that produces legal effects about you.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

- **Run your account and authenticate you** — necessary to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Organise tournaments and leagues, compute rankings** — our legitimate interest in operating organised competitive play (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Discord "Linked Roles"** — provided at your request when you link your account (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).
- **Submit results and winning decklists to vekn.net** — our legitimate interest in maintaining the official competitive record (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Service messages** — where you have given us an email address, to send you messages about the service (Art. 6(1)(b)). We notify you primarily through in-app notices, so email is not required.

## Public competitive record

Tournament results, rankings and submitted decks — together with the player name and VEKN ID attached to them — form a **public competitive record**. We make this data publicly available and share it with third parties, including community-run tools, so that players can analyse trends and results across organised play. This reflects the long-standing practice of organised VTES play and is done on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Third parties who reuse this public data act as their own independent controllers.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services needed to run Archon:

- **Discord** — for sign-in and Linked Roles.
- **vekn.net** — for profile synchronisation and result submission.
- **GitHub** — when a winning decklist is contributed to the public Tournament Winning Decks Archive (TWDA).
- **Email (SMTP)** — to deliver service messages, where you have provided an email.
- **The public / community tools** — for the competitive record described above.

## Data location and international transfers

Archon's servers are hosted within the European Union. Some providers (notably Discord and GitHub) are based outside the UK and the European Economic Area; such transfers rely on UK or EU "adequacy" regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Cookies

Archon uses a **session cookie that is strictly necessary** to keep you signed in. We do not use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is required. Your local data (used to make the app work offline) is stored in your browser, not shared.

## How long we keep it

Account data is kept for the lifetime of your account. Tournament results, decks and rankings are part of the public competitive record and are retained as historical data. Server logs are rotated automatically and kept only briefly.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may **access**, **export** (portability), **rectify** or **erase** your personal data, and **object to** or **restrict** processing based on legitimate interest. Where we rely on consent, you may **withdraw it** at any time. Note that erasing data that forms part of the public competitive record may be limited by our overriding interest in the integrity of that record.

To exercise any right, email **orgplay@blackchantry.com**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — or, if you are in the EU, your national data-protection authority.

## International users

Archon is available worldwide, and we apply this policy to everyone who uses it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights — for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or, in the United States, under state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). To exercise any right available to you, contact **orgplay@blackchantry.com**.

## Age

Archon is intended for users **aged 16 and over**. Younger players may take part only with the permission of a parent or guardian, who must accept the Terms of Service and authorise this processing on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect data from a child under 16 without that authorisation.
