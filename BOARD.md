# Board

A list designed to shrink. **The goal is zero.** Completion is deletion — there is
no closed state, no archive; git history is the record and `git blame` knows a
line's age.

**Order is priority.** Ranking rules, applied top to bottom when two unrelated
lines compete:

1. user-reported defects
2. correctness
3. blocking work and useful refactorings
4. polish
5. new capability

**Hard limit: 15 lines.** Adding a sixteenth forces a drop or a promotion to the
wiki. **No waiting state**: externally-gated work is deferred on the wiki page
that owns it — see [wiki/vekn-decommission.md](wiki/vekn-decommission.md) — with a
named trigger, and returns through `/intake` when the trigger fires.

**Every line must be completable** — if "done" cannot be stated, it is a subject:
promote it to a [wiki](wiki/index.md) page and delete the line. Context lives in
the wiki; asks live here. Bulky context for an in-flight line goes in
`board/<slug>.md`, deleted with the line.

Board changes ride the commit that earns them.

- Make Discord login land an official-created member on their existing record: when the Discord email is verified and no email login matches, fall back to the account's address of record, as magic-link signup already does, and give the registration row import the same fallback. Today a first Discord login always creates a VEKN-less duplicate, even when the addresses match. **Done when:** a member created by an official with an email and never activated, logging in by Discord with that verified email, gets their VEKN record instead of a new account; an import row carrying only that email matches them; and `wiki/access.md` (Discord row, email of record) and `wiki/hazards.md` (which paths check both email fields) say so.
- Drop the in-app toast that fires when a tournament announcement arrives: the banner shows it and subscribed members already get the system notification, so a subscriber with the app open is alerted three times for one message. gh-27. **Done when:** an announcement posted during a running event shows as the banner (and as a push, where subscribed) and raises no toast. Doc-impact: none — no wiki page records the toast.
- Keep a soft-cancelled round out of the player view: a player seated in an earlier round that was cancelled sees it neither as a live table nor under previous rounds, since its tables read `Cancelled`, not `Finished`, and the view keeps them live for the rest of the event (`PlayerView.svelte:180`, `:196`). The organizer Rounds tab already treats cancelled as not in progress. Restoring the round brings it back. **Done when:** after an organizer cancels a non-last round, no player seated in it sees that round anywhere on their tournament view, and a `RestoreRound` shows it again. Doc-impact: none — aligns the view with the soft-cancel rule in `wiki/tournaments.md`.
- Link player names in a tournament's standings and its winner line to the member's public profile, as organizer names already are (`PlayerView.svelte:664,:776,:812`, `FinishedResults.svelte:78`, pattern at `+page.svelte:929`); the organizer roster and seating rows keep their controls and stay unlinked. And let player-facing names wrap instead of truncating on the player's own table and finals table and in the decklist rows (`PlayerView.svelte:506,:590`, `PlayerDecksSection.svelte:363,:367`) — on a phone there is no hover to recover a cut name. And restore the space after the comma between organizer names (`+page.svelte:929`), where Svelte trims the separator's trailing space. gh-20, from a Prince comparing against vekn.net event results; gh-21, a player on Android; gh-30. **Done when:** clicking a name in any standings table or on the winner line of a tournament page opens that member's profile, signed in or not, and no player-facing seat or decklist row ellipsizes a name at 360px width, and several organizers read "Alice, Bob". Doc-impact: none — a link on an existing surface, no recorded decision changes.
- Show a sanction's category, subcategory and reason only to IC, Ethics, the Rulemonger, the NC of its tournament's country, that tournament's organizers and the sanctioned player; every other member sees the sanction's level, dates and tournament but no reason, on the member profile, the members list and tournament pages alike. A display rule, like the existing level filtering — every sanction still syncs to every member in full. **Done when:** a signed-in member viewing another player's Warning sees its level but no category or reason, while that player and the event's organizer both see the reason; and `wiki/tournaments.md` (the Sanctions visibility table and its display-rule paragraph) says who sees the reason.
- Show the day of the week in tournament dates: the tournament list (table and phone rows) and the tournament page's start and finish, in the viewer's language. The league's own date range is out of scope. gh-24, a Prince planning which events to attend. **Done when:** every tournament date in the list and on the tournament page reads with its weekday (e.g. "Sat, 12 Oct 2026") in the viewer's locale. Doc-impact: none — date formatting, no recorded decision.
- Give the member profile's Record tab a full "Events played" list — every finished tournament the member played, newest first, with date, country and final place, whatever its age and whether or not it counted for rating — built from the tournaments members already sync, since today the rating table is the only history and it stops at 18 months and skips unranked events. **Done when:** a member profile, viewed while signed in, lists a tournament the member played more than 18 months ago and an unranked one they played, each linking to its tournament page. Doc-impact: `wiki/product.md` — the Ratings and Hall of Fame paragraph's sentence on what a member profile carries.
- Let a member curate their agenda: hide a tournament that the automatic matching put there, and add one it didn't, from the tournament list and the tournament page. Store both lists on the member record server-side so the personal calendar feed honours them too, and strip both lists from what other members receive, like the calendar token. gh-22, a player on Android; gh-28, long-running online events cluttering the agenda. **Done when:** a hidden tournament disappears from that member's agenda view and personal calendar feed on every device, an added one appears in both, neither list reaches another member's sync, and `wiki/architecture.md` (Calendar, agenda matching) and `wiki/sync.md` (what members receive) say so.
- Log organizers viewing decklists during a running event: an organizer sees a player's card list only through a deliberate "view decklist" action, which records one entry per organizer per deck (first view and its round, offline-queued like any action), shown quietly on the deck row to that event's organizers and to the deck's owner on their own deck. A member viewing their own deck is never logged, and the log stops at finish. It deters casual peeking but doesn't enforce it: the decks are already on the organizer's device. gh-26, a judge wanting to discourage organizer-players from browsing opponents' lists. **Done when:** a second organizer opening a player's list during an ongoing event shows up once, with the round, on that deck row for every organizer of the event and for the player on their own deck; re-opening adds nothing; the record reaches no one else's sync; and `wiki/product.md` (decklists) and `wiki/sync.md` (organizer projection, owner overlay) say so.
