# Board

A list designed to shrink. **The goal is zero.** Completion is deletion — there is
no closed state, no archive; git history is the record and `git blame` knows a
line's age.

**Order is priority.** Ranking rules, applied top to bottom when two unrelated
lines compete:

1. user-reported defects
2. correctness
3. blocking work and useful refactorings
4. polish
5. new capability

**Hard limit: 15 lines.** Adding a sixteenth forces a drop or a promotion to the
wiki. **No waiting state**: externally-gated work is deferred on the wiki page
that owns it — see [wiki/vekn-decommission.md](wiki/vekn-decommission.md) — with a
named trigger, and returns through `/intake` when the trigger fires.

**Every line must be completable** — if "done" cannot be stated, it is a subject:
promote it to a [wiki](wiki/index.md) page and delete the line. Context lives in
the wiki; asks live here. Bulky context for an in-flight line goes in
`board/<slug>.md`, deleted with the line.

Board changes ride the commit that earns them.

- Keep every DB query served by an index: a query added or changed on a growing table (`objects`, `auth_methods`) lands with the index that serves it in `schema.sql`, shown used by `EXPLAIN` in the same diff, or says why a scan is fine (a table that stays small, an ops script). Write the rule in `wiki/architecture.md` (Database access), give the reviewer's charter the matching check (`.claude/agents/reviewer.md`, No new hazard) and name it in `/ship`'s "How to write the code"; then audit the existing queries in `backend/src/` and index the ones that scan a growing table on a request path. An owner correction: the email lookup nearly shipped with two of its three sources unindexed, one of them a scan the codebase already had. **Done when:** the three harness places state the rule, a diff adding a `WHERE` on an unindexed JSONB expression over `objects` is a blocking finding by the charter's own words, and every request-path query on `objects` or `auth_methods` uses an index under `EXPLAIN` on a production-sized copy or is named in the architecture page as a deliberate scan.
- Open a tournament on the player view for a viewer registered to play in it who isn't one of its listed organizers: today anyone the engine lets manage the event, every event for an IC and every event in their country for an NC, lands on the organizer console and must toggle to see their own table. Listed organizers keep the console by default; the toggle stays for both. gh-34, an official playing in events they could manage. **Done when:** an IC registered as a player in an event they don't organize opens it on the player view, a listed organizer who is also registered opens it on the console, and the toggle switches both ways. Doc-impact: none — no wiki page records the default view.
- Show players the full preliminary standings once the finals start, whatever the event's standings mode: at that point places 6 and below are settled and the finalists are public, so Private, Cutoff and Top 10 only make players wait out the final to see their result. The final's own placings still wait for the finish. gh-35, a player waiting two hours to see their result after the last round. **Done when:** in a Private-mode event, a player's view shows the full preliminary standings as soon as the finals are seated, before any finals score, and `wiki/tournaments.md` (the `standings_mode` row: during the preliminaries) and `wiki/sync.md` (the Standings display default) say so.
- Let an organizer renumber tables, for venues whose tables are numbered continuously and for twin events sharing a hall: a "first table number" (default 1) and a "restart numbering in each room" switch (default off: numbering continues across rooms) in the room setup, read by the engine's table label so every surface follows (seating, player view, print, judge call, push). Tables outside any room, and every table when continuing, read first number + position; rooms that restart keep 1…n. Existing events with several rooms get the restart switch set, so no label changes under them. gh-32; gh-33, the EC Day 2 held alongside a twin event for non-qualifiers in one venue. **Done when:** an event with first number 15 and no rooms seats its tables as "Table 15"…; with Main Hall ×5 then Annex ×4 at defaults the Annex reads "Annex 6"…"Annex 9", and with restart on "Annex 1"…"Annex 4"; an event created before the change keeps every label; and `wiki/design.md` (Table labels) and `wiki/tournaments.md` (the room config row) say so.
- Give the member profile's Record tab a full "Events played" list — every finished tournament the member played, newest first, with date, country and final place, whatever its age and whether or not it counted for rating — built from the tournaments members already sync, since today the rating table is the only history and it stops at 18 months and skips unranked events. **Done when:** a member profile, viewed while signed in, lists a tournament the member played more than 18 months ago and an unranked one they played, each linking to its tournament page. Doc-impact: `wiki/product.md` — the Ratings and Hall of Fame paragraph's sentence on what a member profile carries.
- Let a member curate their agenda: hide a tournament that the automatic matching put there, and add one it didn't, from the tournament list and the tournament page. Store both lists on the member record server-side so the personal calendar feed honours them too, and strip both lists from what other members receive, like the calendar token. gh-22, a player on Android; gh-28, long-running online events cluttering the agenda. **Done when:** a hidden tournament disappears from that member's agenda view and personal calendar feed on every device, an added one appears in both, neither list reaches another member's sync, and `wiki/architecture.md` (Calendar, agenda matching) and `wiki/sync.md` (what members receive) say so.
- Log organizers viewing decklists during a running event: an organizer sees a player's card list only through a deliberate "view decklist" action, which records one entry per organizer per deck (first view and its round, offline-queued like any action), shown quietly on the deck row to that event's organizers and to the deck's owner on their own deck. A member viewing their own deck is never logged, and the log stops at finish. It deters casual peeking but doesn't enforce it: the decks are already on the organizer's device. gh-26, a judge wanting to discourage organizer-players from browsing opponents' lists. **Done when:** a second organizer opening a player's list during an ongoing event shows up once, with the round, on that deck row for every organizer of the event and for the player on their own deck; re-opening adds nothing; the record reaches no one else's sync; and `wiki/product.md` (decklists) and `wiki/sync.md` (organizer projection, owner overlay) say so.
- Let an organizer find a player on demand. Players tab: the add-player field's "already registered" match becomes a way to jump to that player, not a greyed-out row: tapping it scrolls to their roster row and highlights it, clearing any filter hiding it, and registered matches rank first. Rounds tab: a search icon opens a field over the event's own players, each result showing its table and seat in the latest round they sit in; tapping one opens that round and highlights the seat, hiding nothing. **Done when:** typing "jose" in the add-player field of a 40-player event with a common-name member base lists the registered José first, and tapping him scrolls to his highlighted row; on the Rounds tab the same search reads "Table 4, seat 2" and tapping it opens his round on the highlighted seat; and `wiki/design.md` ("Unactionable match" reworded from addable to actionable; the workbench noting the Rounds search icon) says so.
- Let an IC anonymize a member holding a VEKN id, as the counterpart of marking one deceased: it wipes name, nickname, contact, city, avatar and sign-in methods, stamps when and by whom, and locks the wiped fields against the VEKN member sync. VEKN id, country, results, ratings, wins and sanctions stay attached, and the member shows as "Anonymized member" everywhere a name appears. Irreversible; never pushed to VEKN. Past TWDA archive credits are out of scope. **Done when:** after an IC anonymizes a member, that member's profile, the members list, rankings, standings and decklists read "Anonymized member" on every device; no other member's sync carries the old name or contact; the next VEKN member sync leaves the record anonymized; the old sign-in no longer logs in; and `wiki/architecture.md` (Account surgery), `wiki/access.md`, `wiki/sync.md` (user row) and `wiki/product.md` (directory) say so.
- Show a deck's comment under its name on the deck view and make it editable in the deck editor, for the owner and for organizers under the same rules as the rest of the list. It is already filled from the deckbuilder's description on link import, kept from pasted text and written to the TWDA entry; today it is never shown and the editor can't change it. It follows the deck's own visibility. **Done when:** a deck imported from a VDB link that has a description shows that text on the deck view; the owner and an organizer can each change it and the change reaches the other's device; the finished event's TWDA entry carries the edited text; and `wiki/product.md` (Decks) names the comment.
