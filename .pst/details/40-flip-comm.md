**archon.vekn.net v2 update**

Dear Princes,

Tomorrow on Aug 7th, I'll deploy the new Archon v2 at archon.vekn.net.
All VEKN accounts, tournament history, leagues, sanctions and ratings carry over.

**You need to log in anew.** Sessions don't carry over. Discord login works exactly as before.
If you logged in with email and password, passwords do not carry over — use the email login link (sent to the same address you had on file).

Everything you know still works the same way.

**What's new**

- Offline mode: run a whole event without internet at the venue; everything syncs back when you're online again.
- Everything is live, no refresh needed, push notifications: round seatings and scores appear on every player's phone immediately
- One synchronized timer for all tables.
- Players can call a judge from their table.
- Announcements: push a message to every player's screen instantly.
- Table rooms: for multi-room venues, seatings tell players which room to go to.
- Staggered rounds: 6, 7 and 11 players tournaments handled transparently
- Promos and Raffles: track promo distribution and draw prize winners

A better showcase for your events:
- Banners and social cards: give your tournament an image; shared links look great on Discord, Facebook and Reddit.
- Personal agenda and calendar feed: everyone can subscribe to the tournaments calendars, tailored to them.
- Standings visibility options: keep standings private, show the finals cutoff score, the top 10, or go fully public between rounds.
- Decklist options: deck lists are editable in-app; share the winner's deck, all finalists', or all decks at the end. TWDA push is automatic.
- Entry-fee tag: track who paid with one click

A home for the community:
- Community tab: global and per-country resources (forums, Discord, WhatsApp groups) and officials directory
- Hall of Fame and rankings filters: live rankings with a country filter, and a brand new Hall of Fame.
- The full rules in-app: rulebook, tournament, judges guide, with deep links you can share to the exact paragraph.
- Five languages (English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian), plus light and dark mode.

**Questions or problems?**

Report anything odd and any improvement idea directly in the app: **click the "Send feedback" button in the "Help" section**.
I'll keep a close eye on things and fix issues fast.

Thank you for everything you do for the game.
